# Changelog

Rate values are kept from 2026-10-01. Earlier quarters are listed with the counts Meta states in its own changelog
(https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/pricing/updates-to-pricing), but no rate rows
are published for them: Meta overwrites its files, its signed download links expire, and no trustworthy archived copy of
the earlier cards exists (the Wayback Machine holds the pages, not the files). Nothing here is reconstructed.

## 2026-10-01 (current)

Meta's rate card changed on 1 October 2026. Source: "Oct. '26 rate card updates" on Meta's pricing page, read 2026-10-27.
Meta states 40 updated rates. This release publishes the full card for all 16 currencies, volume tiers and the country to market table.

- Own rate instead of a regional one: Bangladesh, Iraq, Kazakhstan, Kuwait, Morocco, Nepal, Oman, Sri Lanka, Ukraine.
- Marketing up: Kuwait, Mexico, Morocco, Saudi Arabia, United Arab Emirates, Rest of Asia Pacific, Rest of Middle East.
- Utility and authentication up: Kazakhstan, Kuwait, Morocco, Oman, Pakistan, Peru, South Africa, Ukraine.
- Utility and authentication down: Bangladesh, Iraq, Nepal, Sri Lanka.
- Free service messages: 1,000 per business phone number per calendar month (since 2026-10-01).
- Data check: 16 currencies x 47 markets, 3,296 rate rows, 10,752 volume-tier rows; the 141 countries in the country table are
  all the countries Meta lists, and the member counts per region match Meta's own counts.
